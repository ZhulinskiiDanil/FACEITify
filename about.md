# FACEITify

Every difficulty face turns into a **FACEIT skill level badge**: level cells,
level pages, search results, profiles, leaderboards, the search filters and
RobTop's own levels.

## The badge

- <c-EEEEEE>**1**</c> — Easy
- <c-1CE400>**2-3**</c> — Normal, Hard
- <c-FFC800>**4-7**</c> — Harder, Insane, Easy and Medium Demon
- <c-FF6309>**8-9**</c> — Hard and Insane Demon, a plain Demon among them
- <c-FE1F00>**10**</c> — Extreme Demon
- <c-FE0123>**11**</c>-<c-FFFFFF>**20**</c> — on a demon list, by its place
- <c-9A9A9A>**?**</c> — GD hasn't rated it
- <c-567DFF>**Auto**</c> — no skill to measure, so a badge of its own

The ring fills up with the level and the number takes its tier colour, the way
it does on a real FACEIT profile. A <c-FE1F00>**10**</c> fills it, and the levels
above it keep it full. Opening a level page fills the ring in.

## Above Extreme

A demon on the list is graded by where it sits in it, from a
<c-FE0123>**11**</c> at the very bottom up to a <c-FFFFFF>**20**</c> just short
of the top 100. The list gets harder the higher you climb, slowly along the tail
and then all at once near the top, so the grades follow it: on a list of about
eighteen hundred, an <c-FE0123>**11**</c> is everything past place 1200, a
<c-CC29C8>**15**</c> sits around place 300, and a <c-FFFFFF>**20**</c> is the
handful of places right under the cut.

The colours are FACEIT's own: out of the <c-FE0123>red</c> into
<c-FF019B>magenta</c>, through <c-CC29C8>15</c> into the
<c-1FB2F7>blues</c>, and white at <c-FFFFFF>**20**</c>.

## Challenger

The **top 100 of a demon list** get the Challenger badge with their place in it,
above every grade there is.

## Lists

- **demonlist.org** — the classic list, the default
- **Pointercrate** — the main list, the extended one and the legacy
- **AREDL** — every rated extreme demon, sixteen hundred of them
- **Off** — no requests at all

Each keeps its own cache for an hour, so browsing costs nothing. With no
connection the badges are whatever GD says, and nothing breaks.
